1. Select **Help** at the bottom right.
2. Choose **Report an issue** to write a request, **Chat with support** to open a chat, or **My requests** to see the requests you can open.
3. On a phone the same actions open in a bottom drawer. On a larger screen they open in a panel.

You can also open **Platform Support** in the menu. **Issues** and **Chats** are the two lists there.

Stay signed in to your company. Do not send passwords, payment secrets, or a customer's identity document. Name the screen and what you expected to happen.

Customer tickets under **Support Tickets** are not sent to the platform team from this Help button.
