1. Open **Help**, then **Report an issue**. You can also open **Platform Support** and select **Report an issue** there.
2. The list opens on **Issues**. Enter:
   - **Subject** — at least 3 characters and at most 200.
   - **Category** — General, Account, Billing, Technical, Bug, or Other.
   - **Priority** — Low, Normal, or High. Normal is already selected.
   - **Message** — at most 10,000 characters. Send a message, at least one image, or both.
3. Select **Send**. The new issue opens on its own page.

The list starts on **Mine**. **Reseller** or **Company** is shown only when your account has that extra access.

Filters on the list are status, priority, category, reference, and subject. On **Company**, a reseller filter is also shown.
