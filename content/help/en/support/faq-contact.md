title: Do I use Support Tickets to reach the platform team?
summary: Use the Help button. Support Tickets are for your own customers.
keywords:

translated_from_revision: "1"

No. Use **Help**, then **Report an issue**, **Chat with support**, or **My requests**.

**Support Tickets** in the menu is for tickets with your customers. It is not required for platform help, and those tickets are not the same as a platform support issue.
