A **Help** button stays at the bottom right of the staff dashboard. It opens guides for the current page, search, FAQs, and videos when one is configured. When the account can contact platform support, it also opens **Report an issue**, **Chat with support**, and **My requests**. **Include this page** is off until you turn it on. It sends the path of the page you checked, without the query string, hash, or form values.

The Help button reaches the platform team. **Support Tickets** in the menu is separate. It is for tickets with your own customers. You do not need Support Tickets to contact the platform team.

## Language

Choose **EN** or **বাং** in the header. This browser stores the choice in the `lang` cookie. Articles open in that language. A message you already sent stays in the language you wrote.

## Account and subscription

Company staff see **Subscription** in the menu. A reseller login does not show that item.

Open **Subscription** to see the plan name, status, dates, features, and the company's subscription invoices. If no plan is assigned, the page says that no subscription is assigned to this company. An unpaid subscription invoice can be paid there with **Pay from Wallet** or **Pay using bKash**. This article does not set prices or decide when a plan ends.

If a feature is not included, that screen says the feature is not in the plan. The Help button remains available when the account can contact platform support.

See [Contact platform support](help:support.contact).
