Open **Invoice** in the menu. The page title is **Invoices**.

The columns are **Invoice ID**, **Created**, **Due Date**, **Type**, **Client**, **Zone**, **Note**, **Amount**, and **Status**. **Amount** shows the figure on that row. When part of that figure is paid and part remains, a **Paid** line appears under it. This guide does not explain how the figure or the paid part is calculated.

**Status** shows **Due**, **Paid**, or **Partial**.

Filters are **Invoice ID**, **Created Date**, **Due Date**, **Zone**, **Type**, **Staff**, **Client**, and **Status**. The status filter offers **Due**, **Paid**, and **Partial**.

An **Add** button is shown only when your account can create an invoice. Creating or paying an invoice is not covered here.
