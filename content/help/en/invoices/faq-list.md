The **Invoices** table shows **Invoice ID**, **Created**, **Due Date**, **Type**, **Client**, **Zone**, **Note**, **Amount**, and **Status**.

**Status** is **Due**, **Paid**, or **Partial**. **Amount** is the figure shown on the row. This answer does not explain how that figure is calculated.
