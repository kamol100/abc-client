Open **Add** on Invoices. The form asks for an invoice type, a client, a status that starts as due, and invoice lines. Each line has a description, amount, quantity, and discount. The page shows totals from those lines. This guide does not explain how a recurring client bill is produced, and it does not describe taking a payment.
