Open **Notifications** when the account has notifications.access. The list shows a message, a category, and the time, and it can mark a row read or unread. The API route is named notifications.index, while the seeded permission is notifications.access. Those strings are not the same. If the page is refused, that mismatch is the reason. Settings → Notifications is a different screen and uses company settings.
