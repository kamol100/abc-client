Open **Clients** in the menu. The page title is **Clients**.

Search by name, phone, or PPPoE ID. You can also filter by network, zone, billing type (**Auto**, **Manual**, or **No invoice this month**), and status (**Active** or **Inactive**).

The columns are:

- **SID**
- **ID / Name / Phone**
- **Zone / Address / Network**
- **Termination / Package**
- **Bill / Payment**
- **Online / Info**
- **Status**
- **Actions**

An **Add** button is shown only when your account can add a client. Adding a client is a separate guide. This page does not explain how a bill amount is calculated, and it does not describe changing a client's status.
