title: Which fields stop Add Client from saving?

Saving is rejected without all of these:

- **Client Name** of at least 2 characters
- **PPPoE ID**
- **Password**
- **Network**
- **Network Package**

The form also marks **Phone Number** as required. This answer does not describe billing or discount rules.
