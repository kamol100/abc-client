Open **Clients**, then **Add**. The page title is **Add Client**. The form has three sections: **Basic Information**, **Connectivity**, and **Advance**.

In **Basic Information**, enter:

- **Client Name** — at least 2 characters.
- **PPPoE ID**. When a next SID is available, it is shown beside this field.
- **Password**.
- **Phone Number**. The form marks this field as required.
- **Network**.
- **Network Package**.

Saving needs a client name of at least 2 characters, a PPPoE ID, a password, a network, and a network package. The other fields in **Basic Information**, **Connectivity**, and **Advance** can be filled when you have them.

This guide does not describe billing terms, discounts, or how an invoice amount is produced.
