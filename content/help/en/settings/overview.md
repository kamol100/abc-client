Open **Settings**. General, dashboard, SMS, map, monitoring, OLT, notifications, and invoice print are sections of company-settings.access. They are company preferences, not a customer's record. The older /setting/general, /setting/dashboard, /setting/table, and /setting/themes pages are not this menu. Notification preferences here are not the Notifications inbox.
